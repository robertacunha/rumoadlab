Approach

How I build an ad set

The same four steps for any brand. The ads below are what the steps produce once the buyer, the levers and the test plan are agreed.

01

Learn the buyer first

Audience, price band and product range before the first headline. Here that is people aged 25 to 45 in the Netherlands who run, lift, cycle or play padel and train three or more times a week, choosing between a free plan and Pro at €9.99 a month.

02

Pick the lever, then write

Every ad keeps the brand's existing tone of voice and adds one psychological lever and two emotional triggers to it: contrast, cost per use, mental simulation, risk reversal. The copy follows the lever, so each variant tests a separate idea.

03

Build the system once

Four layouts, one palette, one wordmark. Each layout has its own visual style: the app screen on ink with a dashed volt route for awareness and Stories, a solid volt field for offers, ink with an ultraviolet glow for retargeting cards, and chalk paper for consideration and the conversion ask. Within a round, the layout and the screen style stay fixed, so the message is the only thing a test reads.

04

Test one variable at a time

Each round has one variable, one primary read and one guardrail. The winning message carries forward and the features rotate, so every feature gets read.

Brand

Train with direction

Rumo is Portuguese for direction. The app plans training, recovery and fuel for people who run, lift, cycle or play padel. The look is ink black and chalk with volt for every number that matters and ultraviolet for the mark. The voice is a good coach: direct, calm and specific, always about the session and never about the body.

Method

How a round is run

Meta A/B test for every round

Meta splits the audience so cells never overlap. Flexible ads mix creative on their own and hide which element won, so they come in after a winner exists.

Seven to thirty days per round

Seven days is the floor, so weekday and weekend behaviour both count. The round closes once each cell reaches its conversion target, within 30 days. Results in the first days are unstable, so no winner is called before the round closes.

Even budget, enough results

Cells share the round budget evenly. A winner is called once each cell has roughly 25 to 50 of its target event.

Winner carries forward

The next round holds the winning message and changes the next variable. Every new idea is tested against the current winner.

Optimise on what Meta can learn from

Meta's delivery learns once an ad set reaches about 50 optimisation events in a week. Until subscriptions reach that, rounds optimise on installs or trial starts, and subscriptions are read as the result.

Call winners on confidence

Meta reports how likely the winning cell is to win again. A low score counts as a tie, and the current winner stays.
